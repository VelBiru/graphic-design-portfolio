About Me

I’m a UI/UX and graphic designer who enjoys turning ideas into designs that are clean, purposeful, and easy to understand. I’m interested in both the visual and functional side of design—creating interfaces that feel intuitive while making sure every visual element has a reason to be there.

Areas of focus

What I Do

UI/UX Design

Designing clean and intuitive interfaces for websites and applications, from wireframes and layouts to polished prototypes.

Graphic Design

Creating visual materials such as posters, promotional graphics, social media content, product layouts, and other digital designs.

Visual Design

Working with typography, colors, layouts, imagery, and visual hierarchy to create designs that feel consistent and engaging.
